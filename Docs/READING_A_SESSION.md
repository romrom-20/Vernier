# Read a day, revisit a decision

Start with Overview. The default category cards describe saved follow-up wording; the chart shows when those replies were recorded. Choose a category or hour to see the exact messages and **Why this was counted**. A count is not a task percentage, an attention measure or a grade of thought. [Definitions and coverage](DAILY_RECAP.md).

![Current daily overview from invented records](images/dashboard.png)

The sources in every image here are invented. The native components are current; the conversation interpretations are hand-authored presentation fixtures, not model-evaluation results.

## Find a useful way back in

Below the visual overview, an available contextual reading connects a likely goal, the result reported so far and one specific checkpoint. For example, a successful build can leave an original interaction untested. The reading can suggest replaying that interaction without asserting that you failed to review it elsewhere.

Open **See why this reading was made** to inspect its exact saved interpretation. Supporting messages and review details stay available under their expandable headers. A model challenge and matching quotes do not establish semantic correctness; add your perspective when the account misses your situation.

The compact recap is another entry into the same saved work. Press **Control–Option–Command–V**, or reopen the app icon, then use its source action or the full-recap footer. If yesterday's fuller recap is shown, the action opens yesterday. Pause remains independent.

## Revisit a changed requirement

Some explicit conflicts can surface a question: keep the original requirement, or accept the proposed change? The card shows the earlier boundary and later proposal.

![Current decision review from an invented offline requirement and hosted proposal](images/decision-review.png)

**That was intentional** records your choice. **I want to check this** records an open concern. These responses remain separate from the model's account and suppress repeat ambient nudges for the same pair. A quiet cue is a question about evidence, not a declaration that you were steered.

## Inspect the surrounding conversation

Open the saved exchange to compare your next request with the preceding agent response. Select a map point to inspect a source group; open its original record by ID.

![Current conversation map with an invented before-and-after source comparison](images/conversation-map.png)

The curve follows reading order, with separate speaker lanes. It does not encode time spent, attention or cognitive strain. The map loads at most 500 messages; coverage can be incomplete. A provider conversation can contain several tasks.

## Choose the level of interpretation

Local review is optional and initially off. In **Local model and review settings**, choose an installed engine and check its availability. Automatic revisiting has its own switch and waits for quiet saved work; it does not observe everything on the Mac.

The normal category cards remain wording-based. **Experimental model categories** is a separate, off-by-default mode that retains mixed acts and unread/withheld states. The tested configurations failed their quality gate. Source inspection and clear limitations remain necessary in either mode. [Reasoning](REASONING.md) · [Model results](MODEL_EVALUATION.md).

Your optional daily clarity/energy check-in records your own account. It is separate from the conversation, interpretation and any proposed future assessment of independent capability. The current source is Codex in Conductor; other personal agents are not connected yet.
